# Incremental nature work

Checkpoint: `pre-gen` at `9ff5651f533df13379441cbeb6adfb7d8126356a`.
This extends the accepted [0.6 plan](0.6.0.md); it does not freeze public names,
constructors, schemas, or material defaults.

## Resume, do not rebuild

- MAT070Y already supplies stone, bound road, and forest draw packets. The
  preserved native frame-capture test is RED because its implementation header
  is absent. Sparse road/stone material bindings do not define unsampled-surface
  shading or interpolation. Keep that RED explicit until the consumer contract
  is defined; do not invent a substitute renderer to satisfy it.
- Stone geometry, hierarchical material residency, and camera-path recovery
  are present. Retain their exact identities, passive-energy tests, bounded
  uploads, eviction, and regeneration gates.
- Road standing water and its retained packet adapter already exist in
  `vf-road-water-field.mjs` and `vf-road-water-renderer-packets.mjs`. They are
  private reference work, not an ocean, river, or released water renderer.
- Forest population, growth-aligned wood, canopy material, and coarse tree
  draw packets exist. Nine measured leaf spectra are explicit supported
  species conditions; they do not certify nine complete tree architectures.
- Grass already has demand, GPU-instance, shadow, and filtered-material
  evidence. Reuse these contracts; do not create another grass renderer.
- No terrain, mountain, beach, bush, or flower producer was found in this
  checkpoint's native, web runtime, examples, or release plans.

## Small delivery packets

1. **Measured foliage composition.** Apply the existing nine explicit leaf
   conditions to existing forest bundles, then consume them through the
   unchanged draw-packet adapter. Prove foliage-only changes, exact replay,
   passive energy, bounded demand, and existing rejection diagnostics. This
   adds material variants, not nine measured tree architectures.
2. **Existing producer capture, contract-dependent.** Consume actual geometry and bound material
   bytes. Pin deterministic frame/source versions, per-producer visibility,
   material-to-pixel sensitivity, malformed bindings, and bounded allocation.
   A private CPU oracle is not shared-renderer or GPU parity evidence.
3. **Water consumption.** Connect the existing standing-water field to its
   established road surface consumer. Preserve wetness/depth correlation,
   drainage, passive energy, stable identities, and dry-surface behavior.
   General water needs a separate physical/material contract, not renamed
   road puddles or an independent visual simulation.
4. **Terrain foundation.** Introduce one private demand-refined height/shape
   field with shared boundary samples. Begin with hills, then a mountain
   condition on the same hierarchy. Prove adjacent-cell continuity, stable
   coarse identity, finite normals, camera-budget bounds, and regeneration.
5. **Shore and beach.** Derive shore membership from the same terrain and
   water-level truth. Couple exposed/wet sediment material to that boundary;
   test continuity, water-level changes, and deterministic replay before
   introducing erosion or fluid motion.
6. **Plant families.** Extend the existing species/instance/component hierarchy
   with one additional researched tree architecture, then shrub branching,
   then one flower family. Reuse population placement, growth coordinates,
   canopy/leaf material, demand/residency, and renderer bindings. Add varieties
   only after their geometry and material conditions have independent gates.

Each packet needs RED, GREEN, dependency regressions, and provenance that
distinguishes measurements from artist-authored plausible parameters. Visual
quality requires real renderer captures; optimization requires measured
comparison with the unchanged baseline. No new percentage is inferred from
this roadmap or from source/test counts.

Before exposing any new author controls, submit a compact human decision
packet with examples, defaults, invalid-input diagnostics, and compatibility
impact. Viktor remains the final language-design authority.

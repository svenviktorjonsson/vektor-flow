# VKF 0.6.0 plan: procedural materials and geometry

Status: accepted release direction. Public VKF constructors, property names,
distribution syntax, diagnostics, and interchange APIs remain unfrozen.

## Product goal

An author asks for a stone, tree, forest, road, wood, or another common natural
material and receives a deterministic, plausible object that gains geometry and
surface detail only where the active views require it. The author may skew
meaningful distributions without manually building meshes, textures, random
loops, or level-of-detail systems.

Procedural generation covers geometry and appearance together. A crack may
change shape, color, roughness, porosity, and shadowing from the same underlying
field; it is not a displacement texture unrelated to the material.

0.6.0 builds on the [world-class hybrid renderer roadmap](world-class-hybrid-renderer.md)
and the 0.5 demand-backed vector/compiler foundation. It does not delay the
0.4 useful-UI release or the 0.5 self-hosting fixed point.

## Hierarchical distribution model

Natural variation is neither uniform nor independent. VKF represents it as
conditioned levels:

```text
environment / population
        -> species or material family
             -> object instance
                  -> structural component
                       -> surface region
                            -> microstructure
```

Conceptually, generation follows a factored distribution:

```text
P(population | environment)
P(instance | population, location)
P(component | instance, growth/history)
P(surface | component, position, exposure)
P(microstructure | surface, scale)
```

Examples:

- A forest usually contains spatially clustered trees of related species,
  sizes, age, and health rather than independent random trees.
- Each tree shares species-level branching, bark, and leaf traits but has its
  own crown, trunk, damage, and local response to light and neighbors.
- Wood follows the tree's growth frame: rings, rays, pores, and grain are
  directional solid structure, not a flat repeated image.
- Stones share geological composition and weathering conditions while varying
  in base shape, fracture, erosion, mineral patches, and surface grains.
- A road shares construction layers and aggregate statistics while wear,
  cracks, repairs, dirt, and water respond to traffic, drainage, and exposure.

An author override conditions one or more meaningful levels. Unspecified
levels continue to follow the researched generator instead of resetting to
independent noise.

## Determinism and demand

Every generated fact is addressed by a stable demand key containing generator
version, seed, object identity, hierarchy path, spatial cell, scale, and time.
Generation is counter-based and order-independent: camera direction, thread
count, GPU scheduling, and earlier demands cannot change later results.

The generator first returns conservative coarse structure:

- a closed base shape or skeleton;
- material bounds and aggregate optical properties;
- an error estimate for geometry and each material field; and
- stable refinement handles.

The renderer/compiler refines only visible or otherwise measured handles until
projected geometry, silhouette, displacement, normal, color, and roughness
errors satisfy the active quality budget. Refinement adds vertices/meshlets and
field samples without changing already established large-scale identity.

Coarse and fine representations must transition without cracks or visible
popping. Adjacent cells share boundary samples; temporal refinement uses stable
correspondence, geomorphing, or coverage transitions. Evicted detail can be
regenerated exactly from its key.

## One procedural field graph

A generator is an immutable graph of typed fields and refiners. It may produce:

- occupancy, signed distance, or a parametric/skeletal shape;
- adaptive vertices, edges, faces, and volumes;
- base color, metallic response, roughness, normal, displacement, opacity,
  emission, transmission, and subsurface parameters;
- structural coordinates such as wood growth frame, branch distance, strata,
  road direction, or weather exposure; and
- collision/physics approximations derived from the same shape truth.

Spatially coherent kernels, spectra, grammars, point processes, growth models,
erosion, and measured distributions are reusable graph nodes. White noise is
permitted only when independent samples are actually the intended model.

The runtime may evaluate a field analytically, sample it procedurally in the
shader, cache sparse tiles/bricks, bake a visible region, or refine geometry.
Those are optimizer choices and do not change the generator's meaning.

## Standards and interchange

VKF does not invent another offline texture container. Imported images, meshes,
MaterialX graphs, and common scene formats remain valid source data. The 0.6
material response maps to the compact PBR contract established by the renderer,
with an explicit compatibility path toward OpenPBR. MaterialX is the preferred
research/interchange reference for procedural pattern graphs; VKF retains its
own typed, demand-aware execution semantics.

Export records the generator/version/seed and may optionally bake selected
geometry and texture levels for consumers that cannot execute the procedure.
Research-derived presets retain source, license, units, measurement conditions,
fit method, uncertainty, and generator version.

## Initial researched library

### Stone

- Begin with a closed ellipsoid, superquadric, convex, or implicit base shape.
- Condition macro form on rock class and formation history.
- Add fracture planes, chipped edges, stratification, erosion, mineral regions,
  pores, and grains at separate correlated scales.
- Drive displacement, normal, color, roughness, and optional subsurface response
  from shared geology/weathering fields.

### Tree and forest

- Species owns distributions for architecture, branching angles/order, tropism,
  taper, crown envelope, leaves/needles, bark, growth, and seasonal state.
- Tree structure may combine developmental grammars/L-systems with space
  colonization so local light and neighboring crowns affect the instance.
- Forest placement is a marked spatial point process: positions are points;
  species, age, size, health, and orientation are conditioned marks.
- Environmental fields condition density and species likelihood, while local
  competition enforces plausible spacing and crown response.

### Wood

- Generate a solid growth coordinate field before cutting the visible surface.
- Rings distinguish earlywood/latewood; rays, vessels/pores, fibers, knots, and
  figure follow anatomical directions and branch history.
- The same volume field remains coherent across end grain, face grain, curved
  cuts, broken surfaces, and newly exposed procedural geometry.

### Road

- Represent construction layers, aggregate, binder/concrete, joints, markings,
  repairs, and shoulders along a road coordinate frame.
- Condition cracking, rutting, polishing, dirt, water, snow, and edge breakdown
  on load paths, drainage, slope, climate/exposure, and age.
- Large-scale geometry, surface displacement, albedo, roughness, and wetness
  share the same wear fields.

## Release work packages

### MAT-000 — research and measurement contracts

- Freeze provenance, units, fitting, uncertainty, and licensing records.
- Establish stone, tree/forest, wood, and road reference datasets and visual/
  statistical oracles.
- Separate measured claims from artist-authored plausible presets.

### MAT-010 — deterministic hierarchical distributions

- Add order-independent keyed random variables, conditioned distributions,
  spatial correlation fields, marked point processes, and reproducible sampling.
- Prove CPU/GPU/thread/traversal independence for generator identity.
- Add parameter fitting and goodness-of-fit reports rather than visual guessing.

### MAT-020 — demand-refined geometry

- Add closed coarse shapes, skeleton/implicit sources, error-bounded refinement,
  shared boundaries, stable meshlet identities, caching, and eviction.
- Refine silhouette before hidden interior detail when that minimizes image
  error.
- Keep picking, physics, shadows, and rendering attached to one topology truth.

### MAT-030 — procedural material graph

- Add typed coordinate/field nodes, analytic derivatives, filtered evaluation,
  sparse texture/volume caches, and renderer material outputs.
- Compile one graph to CPU reference, WGSL, and native GPU targets.
- Map the compact renderer material model and validate MaterialX/OpenPBR
  interchange subsets without weakening VKF demand semantics.

### MAT-040 — stone tracer

- Deliver one researched stone family from closed coarse shape through adaptive
  fractured/weathered geometry and correlated PBR fields.
- Demonstrate hundreds of related but non-identical stones under a fixed memory
  and frame budget.

### MAT-050 — tree, wood, and forest tracer

- Deliver one measured tree species with developmental structure, bark, leaves,
  and coherent internal wood.
- Generate an ecologically conditioned forest population with repeatable
  species/age/size spatial statistics.
- Cut a branch/trunk procedurally and preserve volumetric grain continuity.

### MAT-060 — road tracer

- Deliver one layered asphalt or concrete road with correlated aggregate,
  cracks, wear, markings, repairs, dirt, and wetness.
- Prove the same traffic/exposure fields affect geometry and appearance.

### MAT-070 — library and author controls

- Package stable common generators and research receipts.
- Add only the smallest approved VKF construction and distribution-skewing
  surface after concrete stories are reviewed by the Language Design Authority.
- Preserve automatic defaults, deterministic replay, and explicit seed/version
  control.

## Test and benchmark matrix

### Statistical correctness

- Fixed seeds reproduce exact hierarchy keys, topology identities, and accepted
  aggregate statistics regardless of demand order.
- Population/species/instance/surface correlations match versioned target
  distributions within predeclared confidence bounds.
- Skewing one approved parameter changes the intended conditional distribution
  without destroying unrelated correlations.
- Research presets report goodness-of-fit and uncertainty; a plausible image is
  not sufficient evidence.

### Geometry and material correctness

- Every coarse stone is closed, oriented, finite, and refinable.
- Adjacent refinement cells are watertight and stable across camera paths.
- Fine geometry converges toward a high-resolution reference under decreasing
  error tolerance.
- Analytic/filter derivatives agree with finite-difference references.
- Geometry and PBR fields agree at cracks, cuts, rings, knots, and wear regions.
- White-furnace/material energy tests remain green at every procedural level.

### Performance and responsiveness

- Tighten each correctness-gated comparable large-scene visualization row from
  the 0.4 release ratchet to `VKF median / peer median < 0.5x`. This remains a
  future 0.6 target; it must not be claimed or enforced by an unmeasured 0.4
  scaffold.
- A billion potential detail cells allocate only demanded working sets.
- Memory is bounded by the declared cache budget and returns to a steady state
  after camera motion.
- Refinement, shader evaluation, and cache upload have CPU/GPU median, p95, and
  p99 timings plus first-use compilation/stutter evidence.
- UI event-to-state and input-to-present gates remain green while a forest or
  stone field refines in the background.
- Each optimization preserves the statistical, geometry, material, and image
  oracles before performance evidence is considered.

## 0.6.0 acceptance experience

The released compiler builds a VKF scene containing a generated stone family,
one tree species and forest population, coherent wood cuts, and a procedural
road. From a distant camera the scene begins from bounded coarse structure.
Moving close causes only visible geometry/material regions to refine. Moving
away releases detail memory without changing identity. Replaying the same
seed, scene, camera path, and time reproduces the same topology and image within
the target's documented tolerance.

The author can request a common generator and skew a small set of meaningful
distributions. Exact syntax and names are approved later from tiny concrete VKF
examples; internal graph, cache, meshlet, and random-number mechanics are not
public API decisions.

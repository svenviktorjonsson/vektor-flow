# VKF 0.6.0 procedural materials and worlds

Status: foundation in progress. The public VKF material API remains subject to
Viktor's language-design authority; this plan records approved outcomes and
internal acceptance gates without freezing syntax prematurely.

## Release outcome

VKF 0.6.0 makes natural-looking worlds inexpensive to author and responsive to
explore. A user can request a familiar material or object family such as grass,
road, rock, wood, or tree. VKF deterministically generates only the geometry,
surface detail, and material samples demanded by the current views and
measurements, refining detail as it becomes observable.

Variation is hierarchical. Broad distributions describe differences between
object kinds and individuals; narrower conditional distributions describe
variation across one object's surfaces and newly refined geometry. A forest can
therefore favor related species without cloning identical trees, while one rock
keeps coherent large-scale shape and fine-scale surface character.

The same authored seed, stable object identity, hierarchy, level of detail, and
sample coordinate must produce the same result regardless of traversal order,
chunking, CPU worker count, or eligible GPU execution.

## Acceptance gates

### Gate 0: deterministic demand identity

- A length-framed demand key identifies generator/version, seed, domain,
  hierarchy, level of detail, channel, and sample coordinate without ambiguous
  concatenation.
- The counter-based random reference matches published Philox known vectors.
- Sampling is invariant under traversal order, chunking, and worker scheduling.
- CPU and shader representations have parity evidence before GPU generation is
  enabled.

### Gate 1: hierarchical distribution kernel

- Parent distributions condition child variation without requiring eager
  enumeration of all descendants.
- Stable identities preserve already-observed values when unrelated branches
  are added or removed.
- Numeric, categorical, correlated, bounded, and spatially coherent variation
  have deterministic tests.
- The kernel can answer aggregate or bounded demands without materializing a
  complete population when an exact or conservative symbolic answer exists.

### Gate 2: demand-refined geometry and surfaces

- Closed coarse geometry is immediately renderable.
- View-dependent refinement adds vertices and surface samples only where the
  current projection can reveal additional detail.
- Refinement is crack-free across adjacent faces and stable across frames,
  cameras, and worker schedules.
- Geometry, color, normal, roughness, reflectivity, and other material channels
  share stable demand identities without requiring identical resolutions.

### Gate 3: useful procedural library

- At least grass, road, rock, wood, and tree families have documented,
  deterministic presets backed by the common distribution and refinement
  kernels.
- Presets expose meaningful high-level variation rather than engine-specific
  implementation knobs.
- Each family has reference scenes covering individual variation, population
  variation, surface continuity, and close/far level-of-detail transitions.
- Research provenance and licensing are recorded for every empirical model and
  bundled asset.

### Gate 4: renderer and world integration

- Procedural geometry and materials flow through retained `World`, `Layer`,
  `View`, and `Frame` paths rather than a parallel scene system.
- Lighting, shadows, mirror/reflection paths, picking, and UI remain responsive
  while generation work proceeds.
- Invisible or occluded detail is not generated merely because an object is in
  the world.
- CPU is the correctness baseline; eligible GPU work is an internal policy
  choice and has deterministic fallback behavior.

### Gate 5: performance and release evidence

- Small workloads avoid scheduling and streaming overhead when eager execution
  is demonstrably faster.
- Large worlds stay within explicit memory and frame-time budgets while moving,
  zooming, editing seeds, and changing material parameters.
- Benchmarks compare representative VKF flows with relevant eager, lazy, and
  GPU-capable systems; correctness and visible quality are checked before speed.
- Native and browser targets produce equivalent observable results within
  documented numerical tolerances.
- Clean-machine packaging and reproducible evidence close the release.

## Progress accounting

Release percentage is estimated from these gates, weighted by user-visible
outcomes and integration risk rather than raw commit or test counts. Every
progress update reports the total percentage complete and the change since the
previous report in percentage points for 0.4.0, 0.5.0, and 0.6.0.

## Deliberately unresolved public contracts

This plan does not choose public constructors, property names, preset names,
distribution syntax, serialization, or diagnostics. Internal work may proceed
behind existing retained-world and renderer seams. Any genuine public choice is
returned to Viktor as one compact language-design decision packet.
